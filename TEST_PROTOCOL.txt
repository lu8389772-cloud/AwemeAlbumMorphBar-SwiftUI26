AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.52r test protocol

A. Other-profile fullscreen / video right actions
1. Enable the third-party fullscreen playback tweak.
2. Enter another user's profile and open a VIDEO post.
3. Repeat 10 times: open comments -> close comments.
4. Confirm avatar/like/comment/favorite/share remain present after every close.
5. Wait at least 7 seconds after one close and confirm the right column stays present.
6. Swipe to several VIDEO posts and repeat.
7. Repeat on IMAGE posts; image behavior must remain unchanged.
8. Confirm nickname/caption still does not fall to the physical bottom edge after comment dismissal.

B. Clean screen
1. On a normal playable feed, open the avatar menu and choose 一键清屏.
2. Expected immediately: system status bar, top chrome, captions/labels, right actions/avatar, progress/speed chrome where applicable, native bottom bar visual, and LiquidBar disappear.
3. Swipe vertically 5-10 items. Paging must remain normal.
4. Tap the former avatar location once.
5. Expected: the ORIGINAL liquid avatar/base reappears. It must not look like a rectangular/clipped replacement. No other chrome returns.
6. Tap the restored avatar. Expected: the normal full avatar menu opens, including 进入主页, download/copy actions when enabled, and 退出清屏.
7. Choose 退出清屏. Expected: full normal UI and status bar recover.
8. Enter clean screen again, reveal avatar, swipe once. Expected: the avatar hides for the new item and the new item's real avatar-position hot target works.

C. Badge regression
1. Clear all unread Messages. Expected: no red circle at all.
2. Generate unread Messages. Expected: numeric badge appears.
3. Clear unread count again. Expected: badge disappears completely.
4. Friends badge/red-dot behavior remains unchanged.

D. Build/link regression
1. GitHub Actions reaches package stage.
2. No `_OBJC_CLASS_$_AWEPlayInteractionViewController` linker error.
3. No undeclared home-action helper errors.
