AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.56r device test
==================================

Preparation
-----------
1. Disable DYYY/AwemeX and other external fullscreen tweaks.
2. Enable AwemeLiquidBar and leave “内置全屏播放” ON.
3. Fully kill Douyin, then cold launch it.

A. Internal fullscreen
---------------------
1. Home feed: swipe through at least 5 normal videos. Video media should extend to the bottom with no native tab background.
2. Profile/detail: enter another user's profile and open at least 3 videos. Media should be fullscreen while nickname/caption/right controls remain correctly anchored; no 83pt opaque bottom blocker.
3. Comments: on a profile/detail video open and close comments twice. Right-side Liquid Glass actions must remain present.
4. Live pre-stream: swipe through at least 3 live preview cards without entering the room. Each card's “直播中 / 昵称 / 文案” UI must stay at the validated 0.3.1 position; no cumulative upward/downward drift.
5. Image/story work: open a multi-image work and change pages repeatedly. There must be no cumulative downward displacement.
6. Landscape-entry video: verify native horizontal-video fullscreen entry remains usable in normal mode.

B. Clean screen v2
------------------
1. From the avatar menu choose “一键清屏”.
2. Verify these visuals disappear: custom/original bottom bar, top chrome, right action visuals, nickname/caption/anchors, danmaku button/container, native landscape fullscreen-entry button, video/chapter progress bars, image/story page/folio indicators.
3. While still in clean screen, swipe through at least 5 videos. Watch specifically for progress bars, danmaku/fullscreen-entry controls, and bottom bar reappearing during or after reuse.
4. Still in clean screen, open a multi-image work and switch at least 5 pages. Page/folio indicators must not reappear.
5. Use the invisible avatar hit target to open the menu and choose “退出清屏”. All suppressed UI must restore and the bottom bar must be interactive.
6. Repeat enter-clean -> swipe -> exit-clean once more to catch stale generation/reuse issues.

C. Regression checks
--------------------
1. Friends/Messages native badges still update and zero-count badges clear.
2. Avatar menu profile/download actions still work.
3. Comments open/close does not remove right Liquid Glass buttons.
4. No unexpected heat/continuous timer behavior while idle.
