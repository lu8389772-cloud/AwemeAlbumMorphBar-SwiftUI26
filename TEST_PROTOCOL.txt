AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.51c device test

1. Heat / performance
- Launch Douyin, stay on Home 15 s, swipe 8-10 videos, open settings.
- Settings should remain responsive and the device should not immediately heat up as in 1.0.51a.
- With diagnostics enabled, NATIVE_DOWNLOAD_READY and PLAYBACK_HIGHEST should appear roughly once per model instead of hundreds of times.

2. Download
- Open the download Liquid Glass menu on a normal video.
- Tap Save current video.
- Expect DOWNLOAD_ACTION then DOWNLOAD_VIDEO and DOWNLOAD_VIDEO_HTTP in diagnostics.
- Verify the video reaches Photos. If not, export the log; HTTP status/error is now included.

3. Friends
- Enter Friends.
- Verify the custom bar does not cover the caption/bottom recommendation region when the native bar tries to auto-hide.
- Continue until the recent-friends batch is exhausted and RecommendToFriend content appears.
- LiquidBar should remain visible; RecommendToFriend must not be classified as commerce/sensitive.

4. Glass press feedback
- Press/drag/release Search, profile, Like, Comment, Favorite, Share.
- The glass should enlarge on press, stretch slightly with finger movement, and spring back on release.

5. Regression
- Top category group stays centered with left/right top buttons independently hidden.
- Settings gesture still opens.
- Publish/share sensitive routes still hard-suppress the bottom overlay.
