AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.51a device test

1. Basic launch
- Kill Douyin completely and relaunch.
- Confirm LiquidBar loads and Settings can still be opened.
- Confirm auth status remains valid.

2. Native download menu
- Open a normal video and tap the download button above the avatar.
- Opening the menu must NOT move the feed vertically.
- Tap “保存当前视频（智能高清）”; confirm it saves to Photos.
- Open an album/image post; confirm “保存全部图片” shows the image count and saves the images.
- Tap “保存音频”; confirm the system export/share sheet appears after download.
- Tap “复制完整文案”; confirm the full caption is on the pasteboard.
- Test with AwemeX installed and uninstalled; this release must not depend on it.

3. Quality selection
- Keep “优先最高画质播放（可能增加流量）” enabled.
- Open several videos with different quality ladders.
- With diagnostics enabled, inspect PLAYBACK_HIGHEST and DOWNLOAD_VIDEO lines.
- Confirm the selected download stream favors high resolution/high FPS and HEVC when available.
- Disable the switch and confirm subsequent videos no longer log successful PLAYBACK_HIGHEST switching attempts.

4. Friends feed
- Enter Friends and keep scrolling until “近期作品已看完” or the next batch is loaded.
- The LiquidBar must remain visible at the Friends root even if Douyin hides/detaches its native bar.
- Open a profile/search/comment/share route from Friends; the LiquidBar must still hide where appropriate.
- Return to Friends; the LiquidBar must recover.

5. Regression checks
- Top category group remains physically centered with left button only, right button only, both, or neither.
- Search and right action buttons retain Liquid Glass press feedback.
- Message badge option follows the Messages item after custom bottom-bar sorting.
- Publish page opens and closes normally.
- Share/forward lower controls are not blocked by the custom bottom bar.
- Two-finger settings gesture works globally by default; profile-only restriction remains opt-in.

6. Diagnostics
- If a download fails, export diagnostics immediately after one attempt.
- Useful markers: NATIVE_DOWNLOAD_READY, DOWNLOAD_VIDEO, PLAYBACK_HIGHEST, TAB_FORWARD_END, VISIBILITY_MASK.
