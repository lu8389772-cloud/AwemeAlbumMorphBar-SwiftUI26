AwemeLiquidBar 1.0.37 release test protocol

1. Settings title and installed package version both show 1.0.37.
2. Confirm “顶部与右侧按钮色调模式” is available on both iOS 26 and iOS 27 and defaults OFF after a clean install.
3. With the switch OFF, menu/Search/right-side controls use the existing pure Clear Glass material.
4. With the switch ON, those same controls rebuild with regular/tinted Liquid Glass; Automatic SF Symbol foreground is left to the system material.
5. Toggle the switch while the Home feed is visible. Existing hosts must refresh immediately without restarting Douyin.
6. Test Automatic, White, Black, Accent and Custom SF Symbol colour modes in both glass materials. Explicit modes must remain explicit.
7. Enable custom PNG mode. PNG colours, alpha and aspect ratio must be identical in Clear and tinted glass modes. A 30 x 30 PNG must remain 30 x 30; only artwork exceeding its doubled ceiling may be reduced.
8. Selected Like remains pink and selected Favorite remains yellow in Automatic mode.
9. Top and bottom Search remain mutually exclusive.
10. The independent bottom bar, its iOS 27 force-dark option, authorization and route visibility mirroring remain unchanged.
